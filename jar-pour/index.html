Jar Pour

A calm colour-sorting puzzle for Android.

How to play

  • Tap a jar, then tap where to pour.
  • You can pour onto the same colour or into an empty jar.
  • Fill every jar with a single colour to clear the level.

Features

  • Endless levels, each checked to be solvable
  • Difficulty that adapts to how you play
  • Colour-blind friendly symbols on every colour
  • Undo, restart, hints and a spare jar

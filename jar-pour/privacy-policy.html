Jar Pour — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Jar Pour ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Asad Fareed (Coder111 Apps) ("we"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars and difficulty settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers, we do not have accounts, and we never receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions with ads, diagnostics and performance data

This data is processed by Google under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings on the app's home screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
